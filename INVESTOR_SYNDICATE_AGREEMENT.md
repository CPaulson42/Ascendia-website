## PART II: OPERATING MANDATE & THE ASCENDIA CREED

### 2.1 The Ascendia Operating Standard
The Manager shall deploy and manage Syndicate capital in strict conformity with the **Ascendia Manifesto and Operating Creed**:

1. **Chartists, Not Gamblers:** The Manager trades exclusively based on confirmed price action, market structure, volume expansion, and technical confluence. *If there is no setup, there is no trade.*
2. **Capital Preservation First, Profit Second (The 2.0% Risk Rule):** Downside protection governs all capital deployment. Under no circumstances shall any single trade position risk more than two percent (2.0%) of the Pool's total equity on a stop-loss basis. Hard stop-loss orders are submitted synchronously upon trade entry.
3. **Clarity Over Chaos:** The Manager filters noise and focuses on high-conviction breakout trends and institutional liquidity patterns with a minimum 1 : 3.5 Risk-to-Reward profile.
4. **Discipline Over Speculation:** *"We are not here to get rich quick. We are here to get rich for sure."*

### 2.2 Asset Allocation Framework
The Pool shall allocate capital according to the following strategic parameters:
* **Macro Trend Momentum (approx. 50%):** Core digital asset leaders (e.g., BTC, ETH, SOL) traded on higher timeframes (4H/1D) with trailing profit protection.
* **High-Conviction Breakouts (approx. 25%):** High-volume ecosystem leaders and range breakouts satisfying the Ascendia Horizon Engine™ underwriting filters.
* **Secured Stables & Yield Buffer (approx. 25%):** Cash and institutional stablecoin reserves deployed in audited lending/staking protocols (generating 8%–12% baseline yield) to maintain downside liquidity and dry powder for pristine setups.

## PART III: FEES, ALLOCATIONS, & HIGH-WATER MARK

### 3.1 Management Fee
The Manager shall receive an annualized **Management Fee of two percent (2.0%)** of the Investor’s Capital Account balance, accrued monthly and deducted proportionally from the Pool to cover data telemetry, institutional research, operational infrastructure, and administrative expenses.

### 3.2 Performance Allocation (Carried Interest)
The Manager shall be entitled to a **Performance Fee of twenty percent (20%)** of net trading profits generated on the Investor’s Capital Account (**80% to Investor / 20% to Manager**).

### 3.3 Strict High-Water Mark Protection
The Performance Fee is subject to a strict **High-Water Mark (HWM)**:
* No Performance Fee shall be accrued, calculated, or paid unless the net asset value of the Investor’s Capital Account exceeds its highest previous valuation (net of any prior withdrawals).
* In the event of an unprofitable trading period, the Manager shall earn zero performance compensation until all cumulative net losses have been fully recouped and a new net profit peak is established.

## PART IV: CAPITAL CONTRIBUTIONS, LOCK-UP, & WITHDRAWALS

### 4.1 Initial Lock-Up Period
To allow systematic swing trading setups to mature and avoid premature liquidation of open positions, all capital contributions are subject to an initial **three (3) month (90-day) lock-up period** commencing on the date capital is deployed into active trading.

### 4.2 Redemption & Withdrawal Rights
* Following the expiration of the initial 90-day lock-up period, the Investor may redeem all or a portion of their Capital Account at the end of any calendar quarter (the "Redemption Date").
* The Investor must provide written notice of redemption to the Manager at least **thirty (30) calendar days** prior to the applicable Redemption Date.
* Redemptions shall be processed and remitted within ten (10) business days following the end of the applicable redemption quarter in USD wire or stablecoin (USDC/USDT), at the Investor's election.

### 4.3 General Partner Capital Commitment
The General Partner and its principals affirm that they maintain an aggregate personal capital commitment of not less than **ten percent (10.0%)** of total Pool capital, invested alongside the Investor on identical performance terms.

### 5.1 Custody & Asset Security
* Reserve Pool assets shall be safeguarded using institutional-grade multi-signature cold storage infrastructure (e.g., Gnosis Safe / Fireblocks) requiring multiple cryptographic key approvals for any outbound capital movement.
