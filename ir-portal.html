Absolutely. Many partners start with $10k or $20k to experience our execution and monthly distributions firsthand, then scale up to $50k or $100k as capacity allows.
Q — "How does the 2% risk rule protect against crypto crashes?"
Because each trade's maximum stop-out loss is capped at 2% of total equity, even an extreme flash-crash event only impacts a single trade's defined risk. We never hold un-hedged bags into multi-month drawdowns.
Q — "How do payouts work?"
You can choose automatic bimonthly payouts sent directly to your registered wallet/bank, or enable auto-compounding to maximize exponential growth.
Q — "What are the fees?"
We operate on a 2.0% annual management fee and a transparent 80/20 profit split across all tiers with a strict High-Water Mark. If we don't generate net profit, we earn zero performance fee.
